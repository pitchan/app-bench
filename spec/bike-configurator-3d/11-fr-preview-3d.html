11. Functional requirements — 3D bike preview

bike-preview shows the configured bicycle in three dimensions. The customer turns it with the pointer or the keyboard, and every part added or removed appears or disappears on the model. The bicycle, the camera and the projection are defined below with exact numbers, so that what the page shows can be computed independently and compared pixel by pixel.

11.1 Coordinates and units

  • Lengths are in millimetres, angles in degrees.
  • The world axes: x runs along the bicycle, from the rear axle towards the front; y points up, the ground being y = 0; z points to the side of the bicycle that faces the camera at yaw 0.
  • (a, b, c) is a point or a vector. cos and sin take degrees.

11.2 Primitives

The model is made only of capsules and boxes.

Primitive Defined by Solid made of
capsule cap(P, Q, r) two points P, Q and a radius r every point whose distance to the segment [P, Q] is at most r
box box(Min, Max) two corners, the box being aligned on the world axes every point (x, y, z) with Min.x ≤ x ≤ Max.x, Min.y ≤ y ≤ Max.y, Min.z ≤ z ≤ Max.z

11.3 Model parameters

Parameter Value
R, wheel radius 350 for 27.5" wheels, 375 for 29" wheels
L, seat tube length 430 for frame S, 470 for M, 510 for L, 550 for XL; 470 while no frame is selected
C, frame color the hex value of the selected color (02-catalog-and-rules)

11.4 Key points

Point Coordinates
RA, rear axle (0, R, 0)
FA, front axle (1080, R, 0)
BB, bottom bracket (420, 300, 0)
ST, top of the seat tube (420 − 0.25·L, 300 + L, 0)
HT, top of the head tube (1000, 340 + L, 0)
HB, bottom of the head tube (1020, 220 + L, 0)
SP, top of the seat post ST + (−15, 60, 0)
HS, top of the stem HT + (−60, 60, 0)

11.5 Parts

11.6 Camera and projection

The camera turns around the target T = (540, 450, 0) at the distance D = 3600. Its position is set by the yaw θ and the pitch φ.

Quantity Definition
camera position E T + D·(sin θ·cos φ, sin φ, cos θ·cos φ)
forward f `(T − E) /
right r `(f × (0, 1, 0)) /
up u r × f
focal length F (H / 2) / tan(15), where H is the height of bike-preview in CSS pixels (vertical field of view 30°)

W and H are the width and the height of the content box of bike-preview in CSS pixels. A point (px, py) of bike-preview is measured in CSS pixels from the top left corner of that content box.

  • Projection of a world point Q: with d = Q − E, the point is shown at px = W/2 + F·(d·r)/(d·f) and py = H/2 − F·(d·u)/(d·f).
  • Ray of a point (px, py): the half-line from E in the direction f + ((px − W/2)/F)·r − ((py − H/2)/F)·u.
  • Part seen at (px, py): the present part that owns the primitive whose solid the ray of that point meets first (smallest distance from E). When the ray meets no solid, no part is seen there.

At yaw 0 and pitch 0 the camera is on the +z side, the front of the bicycle on the right of the image.

11.7 Requirements

FR-PRV-01 Preview element and state

  • Statement: The system shall render bike-preview as a <canvas> element, focusable (tabindex="0"), with role="img" and the accessible name Bike preview. The system shall expose two functions:
    • window.bikePreviewState() returns a new plain object { yaw, pitch, parts }: yaw is θ in degrees, from 0 included to 360 excluded; pitch is φ in degrees; parts is the array of the names of the present parts (11.5), in the order of the table of 11.5.
    • window.bikePickAt(px, py) returns the name of the part seen at (px, py) (11.6), or null when no part is seen there.
  • Rationale: The model can be checked by its numbers as well as by its pixels.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Initial state
  When the page loads
  Then bike-preview is a canvas with role "img" named "Bike preview"
  And bikePreviewState() returns yaw 0, pitch 10 and parts "frame, saddle, wheel-rear, wheel-front"

FR-PRV-02 Initial camera

  • Statement: When the page loads, the system shall set the yaw to 0 and the pitch to 10. The configuration changes of files 05 to 08 (frame, wheels, color, accessories, new configuration) shall not change the yaw or the pitch.
  • Rationale: Every customer starts from the same side view, and the view the customer chose is kept while they configure.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: View kept while configuring
  Given the user has dragged bike-preview 50 px to the right
  When the user checks "Lights"
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 45 and pitch 10

FR-PRV-03 Parts follow the configuration

  • Statement: The system shall keep the present parts, the parameters R and L and the frame color C (11.3 and 11.5) equal to the current configuration at all times, in bikePreviewState(), in bikePickAt and in the drawing.
  • Rationale: The customer sees the bike they are building.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Parts added and removed
  When the user checks "Rear rack"
  Then bikePreviewState() returns parts "frame, saddle, wheel-rear, wheel-front, mudguard-rear, mudguard-front, rack"
  When the user unchecks "Mudguards"
  Then bikePreviewState() returns parts "frame, saddle, wheel-rear, wheel-front"

FR-PRV-04 Part seen at a point

  • Statement: For every point (px, py) of bike-preview that is not exempt, in every view, window.bikePickAt(px, py) shall return the part seen at that point as 11.6 defines it. A point (px, py) is exempt when the part seen (or no part) is not the same at the point and at each of the eight points (px + a, py + b), a and b taken in −1.5, 0, 1.5, not both 0.
  • Rationale: Parts in front hide the parts behind them, as on a real bicycle.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Kickstand in front, then hidden by the rear wheel
  Given the user checks "Kickstand"
  When the projection of the point (320, 75, 135) is (px, py)
  Then bikePickAt(px, py) returns "kickstand"
  When the user presses ArrowLeft 10 times on bike-preview
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 210
  And bikePickAt at the new projection of (320, 75, 135) returns "wheel-rear"

FR-PRV-05 Drawing

  • Statement: The system shall draw the model in bike-preview as 11.6 defines it, at a device pixel ratio of 1. Pixel (i, j) of bike-preview is the screenshot pixel whose center is the point (i + 0.5, j + 0.5). For each pixel whose center is a point where a part is seen and that is not exempt (FR-PRV-04), the pixel shall have the color of that part shaded: there is one factor s from 0.4 to 1 such that each of its red, green and blue channels differs by at most 6 from s times the same channel of the part color. For each pixel whose center is a point where no part is seen and that is not exempt, the pixel shall be #EDEDF2 within 6 on each channel, in both color schemes.
  • Rationale: What the page reports is what the customer sees.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Frame color on the canvas
  Given the content box of bike-preview is 310 × 260 CSS pixels (wide viewport)
  When the user selects card-color-red
  Then pixel (179, 124), at the middle of the down tube (BB + HB) / 2, has the color "#C8102E" shaded
  And pixel (2, 2) is "#EDEDF2"

FR-PRV-06 Turning with the pointer

  • Statement: While the primary pointer button is held after a pointerdown on bike-preview, the system shall, for each pointer move of dx CSS pixels to the right and dy CSS pixels down, add 0.9·dx to the yaw and 0.5·dy to the pitch. The yaw is then brought between 0 included and 360 excluded by adding or removing whole turns of 360; the pitch is then kept between −20 and 60. A move after the button is released shall change nothing. The page shall not scroll or select text during the drag.
  • Rationale: The customer looks at the bike from any side.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Drag
  When the user drags bike-preview 100 px to the right and 40 px down
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 90 and pitch 30
  When the user drags bike-preview 100 px to the left and 200 px down
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 0 and pitch 60
  When the user drags bike-preview 30 px to the left
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 333 (within 0.01)

FR-PRV-07 Turning with the keyboard

  • Statement: While bike-preview has the focus, the system shall add 15 to the yaw on ArrowRight and remove 15 on ArrowLeft, add 5 to the pitch on ArrowUp and remove 5 on ArrowDown, with the same bounds as FR-PRV-06, and prevent the default action of these four keys. Home shall bring the yaw back to 0 and the pitch to 10.
  • Rationale: The preview can be turned without a pointer.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Keys
  Given bike-preview has the focus
  When the user presses ArrowLeft
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 345
  When the user presses ArrowUp 20 times
  Then bikePreviewState() returns pitch 60
  When the user presses Home
  Then bikePreviewState() returns yaw 0 and pitch 10

FR-PRV-08 Refresh

  • Statement: Within 500 ms of any change of the configuration, of the yaw, of the pitch or of the size of bike-preview, the system shall show the new state on the canvas, and bikePreviewState() and bikePickAt shall return it at once. The canvas shall then stay unchanged until the next change.
  • Rationale: The image always matches the configuration, and the page settles.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Wheels changed
  When the user selects card-wheels-29
  Then within 500 ms the screenshot of bike-preview matches the model with R = 375
