9. Design requirements

Values are computed values read in the browser (04-design-system, 4.2). "Wide" means a viewport width of 1024 px or more; "narrow" means less than 1024 px. All boxes use box-sizing: border-box unless stated otherwise.

9.1 Tokens

DR-TOK-01 Tokens declared

  • Statement: The system shall declare on :root every token of 04-design-system, section 4.1, with the value of the active theme.
  • Rationale: Tokens are the single source of every color, length and duration.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Light theme tokens
  Given the color scheme is light
  When the page loads
  Then the value of --color-accent on :root is "#0E6B58" in any letter case
  And the value of --radius-card on :root is "12px"

DR-TOK-02 Page base styles

  • Statement: The system shall give body a margin of 0, a background color --color-bg, a text color --color-text, a font size of 16px and a line height of 24px.
  • Rationale: Every component inherits one base.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Body
  Given the color scheme is light
  Then body has background-color "rgb(246, 244, 240)", color "rgb(27, 26, 24)", font-size "16px", line-height "24px", margin "0px"

9.2 Layout

DR-LAY-01 Wide layout

  • Statement: While the viewport is wide, the system shall lay out main with a maximum width of 1200px including a horizontal padding of 24px on each side, centered in the viewport, and place configurator and summary side by side with their tops aligned: summary on the right, 360px wide, 32px from configurator, and configurator taking the remaining width.
  • Rationale: The price stays next to the choices on a desktop screen.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: 1280 px viewport
  Given the viewport is 1280 × 800
  Then the configurator box starts at x = 64 and is 760 px wide
  And the summary box starts at x = 856 and is 360 px wide
  And configurator and summary have the same top

DR-LAY-02 Sticky summary

  • Statement: While the viewport is wide, the system shall keep summary in view when the page scrolls, with position: sticky and a top of 24px.
  • Rationale: The customer sees the price while scrolling through options.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Scrolled page
  Given the viewport is 1280 × 600
  Then summary has computed position "sticky" and top "24px"
  When the window scrolls until the top of the configurator box is at y = −100 in the viewport
  Then the top of the summary box is at y = 24 in the viewport

DR-LAY-03 Narrow layout

  • Statement: While the viewport is narrow, the system shall lay out main with a horizontal padding of 16px, place summary below configurator with a vertical gap of 32px, give both the full content width, and give summary position: static.
  • Rationale: On a phone the page reads top to bottom.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: 375 px viewport
  Given the viewport is 375 × 812
  Then configurator and summary both start at x = 16 and are 343 px wide
  And the top of summary is 32 px below the bottom of configurator

DR-LAY-04 Section order and headings

  • Statement: The system shall show the seven sections in the order of 03-ui-contract, section 3.1, separated by a vertical gap of 16px, each with an <h2> whose text is, in order, 1. Rider, 2. Frame, 3. Wheels, 4. Drivetrain, 5. Color, 6. Accessories, 7. Quantity, with a font size of 20px, a font weight of 600 and a top margin of 0.
  • Rationale: Numbered steps tell the customer where they are.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Headings
  Then the h2 of section-color reads "5. Color" with font-size "20px" and font-weight "600"
  And the top of section-wheels is 16 px below the bottom of section-frame

DR-LAY-05 Card grids

  • Statement: The system shall arrange the cards of each section in a grid with a gap of 12px in both directions and equal column widths (each column minmax(0, 1fr), so that card content never changes a column width), with these column counts:
Section Wide Narrow
Frame 4 2
Wheels 2 2
Drivetrain 3 1
Color 4 2
Accessories 2 2
  • Rationale: Cards of the same group are compared side by side.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Frames on a wide screen
  Given the viewport is 1280 × 800
  Then the four frame cards have the same top and the same width
  And the left edge of card-frame-M is 12 px after the right edge of card-frame-S

Scenario: Frames on a phone
  Given the viewport is 375 × 812
  Then card-frame-L is below card-frame-S, with the same left edge

9.3 Components

DR-CMP-01 Section panels

  • Statement: The system shall give each section a background --color-surface, a 1px solid --color-border border, a radius of --radius-card and a padding of 24px.
  • Rationale: Each step reads as one panel.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Panel
  Given the color scheme is light
  Then section-rider has background-color "rgb(255, 255, 255)", border-top "1px solid rgb(220, 214, 204)", border-radius "12px", padding "24px"

DR-CMP-02 Option cards

  • Statement: The system shall give each option card, in its default state, a background --color-surface, a 1px solid --color-border border, a radius of --radius-card, a padding of 16px and a pointer cursor; its [data-role="name"] a font size of 16px, a font weight of 600 and the color --color-text; its [data-role="price"] a font size of 14px, the color --color-muted and font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • Rationale: Cards share one look so the state changes stand out.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Default card
  Given the color scheme is light
  Then card-drivetrain-sport has border "1px solid rgb(220, 214, 204)", padding "16px", cursor "pointer"
  And its price has font-size "14px", color "rgb(103, 98, 91)", font-variant-numeric "tabular-nums"

DR-CMP-03 Color swatches

  • Statement: The system shall draw each [data-role="swatch"] as a 32px × 32px circle (border-radius: 50%) with the color's hex value as background color and a 1px solid --color-border border.
  • Rationale: The customer sees the actual paint.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Sand swatch
  Then the swatch of card-color-sand is 32 × 32 px with background-color "rgb(216, 199, 166)" and border-radius "50%"

DR-CMP-04 Recommended badge style

  • Statement: The system shall give the badge a background --color-accent, the text color --color-accent-contrast, a radius of --radius-pill, a font size of 14px, a font weight of 600, a padding of 2px 8px and white-space: nowrap, and shall position it absolutely, horizontally centered on its card, with its vertical center on the card's top border edge, so that it takes no space inside the card and never changes the card's size.
  • Rationale: The recommendation is visible at a glance.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Badge
  Given the color scheme is light and the height is 178
  Then the badge of card-frame-L has background-color "rgb(14, 107, 88)", color "rgb(255, 255, 255)", padding "2px 8px"
  And the vertical center of the badge is at the top edge of card-frame-L, within 1 px
  And the horizontal center of the badge is at the horizontal center of card-frame-L, within 1 px

DR-CMP-05 Primary button

  • Statement: The system shall give order-button the full content width of summary, a height of 48px, no border, a radius of --radius-control, a font size of 16px, a font weight of 600, a background --color-accent and the text color --color-accent-contrast; while disabled, a background --color-disabled-bg, the text color --color-muted and a not-allowed cursor.
  • Rationale: The one action of the page is the most visible element.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Disabled then enabled
  Given the color scheme is light and the viewport is 1280 × 800
  When the page loads
  Then order-button is 310 px wide and 48 px high, with background-color "rgb(236, 232, 225)" and cursor "not-allowed"
  When the user fills height-input with "178"
  Then order-button has background-color "rgb(14, 107, 88)" and color "rgb(255, 255, 255)"

DR-CMP-06 Secondary buttons

  • Statement: The system shall give promo-apply, promo-remove, qty-decrease, qty-increase and confirmation-new a transparent background, a 1px solid --color-border border, the text color --color-text, a height of 40px and a radius of --radius-control; qty-decrease and qty-increase shall be 40px wide. While disabled, these buttons shall have an opacity of 0.45 and a not-allowed cursor.
  • Rationale: Secondary actions stay quiet next to the primary one.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Quantity buttons
  When the page loads
  Then qty-decrease is 40 × 40 px with background-color "rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)", opacity "0.45", cursor "not-allowed"
  And qty-increase has opacity "1"

DR-CMP-07 Summary typography

  • Statement: The system shall style summary like a section panel (DR-CMP-01), draw bike-preview with no border and no padding, at the full content width of summary and 260px high, so that its content box is that full width by 260px (310 × 260 on a wide viewport), and lay out each summary line as one row: label on the left, value aligned on the right edge of the line, both on a single line (white-space: nowrap); a label too long for the row is cut with text-overflow: ellipsis and never pushes the value out of the row. Values use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums. The label and value of summary-line-total have a font size of 28px and a font weight of 700, and the line has a 1px solid --color-border top border. Discount values (qty-discount, promo) have the color --color-accent.
  • Rationale: Prices line up in a column; the total is the first thing read.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Total line
  Given the configuration of example P1 and the color scheme is light
  Then the value of summary-line-total has font-size "28px" and font-weight "700"
  And the value of summary-line-qty-discount has color "rgb(14, 107, 88)"
  And the right edge of every summary value is at the same x, within 1 px

DR-CMP-08 Field errors

  • Statement: The system shall give height-error and promo-error the color --color-danger and a font size of 14px, and give height-input a border color --color-danger while height-error is visible.
  • Rationale: A wrong value is noticed where it was typed.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Height error
  Given the color scheme is light
  When the user fills height-input with "140"
  Then height-error has color "rgb(180, 35, 24)" and font-size "14px"
  And height-input has border-top-color "rgb(180, 35, 24)"

DR-CMP-09 Promo status color

  • Statement: While promo-status is visible, the system shall give it the color --color-accent while the applied code is active and --color-muted while it is not.
  • Rationale: The customer sees at a glance whether the code works.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Inactive then active
  Given the color scheme is light and the configuration of example P6 with SPRING10 applied
  Then promo-status has color "rgb(103, 98, 91)"
  When the user checks "Kickstand"
  Then promo-status has color "rgb(14, 107, 88)"

DR-CMP-10 Notice placement and style

  • Statement: While notice is visible, the system shall display it fixed 24px above the bottom of the viewport, horizontally centered, at most 480px wide, with a background --color-surface, a 4px solid --color-accent left border, a padding of 12px 16px and a radius of --radius-control.
  • Rationale: Automatic changes are noticed without blocking the page.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Notice placement
  Given the viewport is 1280 × 800
  When the user checks "Rear rack"
  Then the bottom of notice is at y = 776 and its center is at x = 640, within 1 px

9.4 States

DR-STA-01 Card hover

  • Statement: While the pointer is over an option card that is neither selected nor disabled, the system shall give it a border color --color-muted. Hovering a selected or a disabled card shall not change its border.
  • Rationale: The pointer shows what can be clicked.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Hover
  Given the color scheme is light
  When the pointer hovers card-wheels-29
  Then card-wheels-29 has border-top-color "rgb(103, 98, 91)"
  When the pointer hovers card-color-glacier
  Then card-color-glacier has border-top-color "rgb(220, 214, 204)"

DR-STA-02 Card selected

  • Statement: While an option is selected, the system shall give its card a 2px solid --color-accent border and show its [data-role="check"] element.
  • Rationale: The current choice is unmistakable.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Selected wheels
  Given the color scheme is light
  When the page loads
  Then card-wheels-275 has border-top "2px solid rgb(14, 107, 88)" and its check is visible
  And the check of card-wheels-29 is not visible

DR-STA-03 Card disabled

  • Statement: While an option is disabled, the system shall give its card an opacity of 0.45 and a not-allowed cursor, and show its [data-role="reason"] element.
  • Rationale: An unavailable option is visible and explained.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Glacier
  Then card-color-glacier has opacity "0.45" and cursor "not-allowed"

DR-STA-04 Keyboard focus

  • Statement: While the input of an option card has keyboard focus (:focus-visible), the system shall draw on the card a 2px solid --color-accent outline with an outline offset of 2px. While any button has keyboard focus, the system shall draw the same outline on the button.
  • Rationale: Keyboard users see where they are, even though the inputs are visually hidden.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Tab into the wheels group
  Given the color scheme is light
  When the user moves the focus with the Tab key to the radio "27.5\" wheels"
  Then card-wheels-275 has outline-style "solid", outline-width "2px", outline-color "rgb(14, 107, 88)" and outline-offset "2px"

DR-STA-05 Stable card size

  • Statement: The system shall keep each option card's width and height identical in every state (default, hover, focus, selected, disabled), so that a state change never moves another element.
  • Rationale: Thicker selected borders must not make the layout jump.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Selecting a card
  Given the viewport is 1280 × 800
  When the user selects card-drivetrain-sport
  Then the bounding boxes of card-drivetrain-sport and card-drivetrain-pro are unchanged

9.5 Theme and motion

DR-THM-01 Dark theme

  • Statement: While the media query (prefers-color-scheme: dark) matches, the system shall use the dark values of every color token; otherwise it shall use the light values. No control switches the theme.
  • Rationale: The page follows the customer's system setting.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Dark
  Given the color scheme is dark
  When the page loads
  Then body has background-color "rgb(18, 17, 16)"
  And section-frame has background-color "rgb(29, 27, 25)"
  And card-wheels-275 has border-top-color "rgb(59, 196, 165)"

DR-MOT-01 Transitions and reduced motion

  • Statement: The system shall give every option card and every <button> a computed transition-property that covers border-color, background-color and color (either the single value all, or a list containing these three properties), with a computed transition-duration of --duration-fast (0.15s) for every entry of that list. Option cards and buttons shall not transition any property with another duration. While the media query (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) matches, every transition and animation duration in the page shall be 0s.
  • Rationale: State changes are smooth, except for customers who ask for no motion.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Normal motion
  Then the transition-property of card-frame-S is "all" or contains "border-color", "background-color" and "color"
  And every comma-separated entry of the transition-duration of card-frame-S and of order-button is "0.15s"

Scenario: Reduced motion
  Given reduced motion is requested
  Then every comma-separated entry of the transition-duration of card-frame-S and of order-button is "0s"
