1. Product overview

1.1 Purpose

Tally Book is a small spreadsheet workbook: one to five sheets, each of 8 columns and 12 rows. The user types numbers, dates, texts and formulas into cells. Formulas refer to other cells, of the same sheet or of another one, directly or through defined names, and are recalculated as soon as anything changes. The user can add, rename and delete sheets, insert and delete rows and columns, copy, cut and paste cells, sort and fill them, find and replace contents, exchange a sheet as CSV text, choose a number format, and undo or redo every change. The workbook is kept in the browser and comes back when the page is loaded again.

1.2 Scope

In scope:

  • A workbook of one to five named sheets, shown one at a time, with tabs to add, rename, delete and switch sheets.
  • For each sheet, a grid of 96 cells with its own selection (one cell or a rectangle of cells).
  • A formula bar that shows and edits the content of the active cell.
  • Numbers, dates, texts, logical values and formulas, with defined error values.
  • A formula language with arithmetic, comparison, concatenation, cell references, ranges, references to other sheets, defined names and 47 functions.
  • Detection of circular references, across sheets.
  • Four number formats, one of them for dates.
  • Up to ten defined names, each for a cell or a rectangle of cells.
  • Inserting and deleting rows and columns, with the rewriting of the references that point to moved or removed cells, in every sheet.
  • An internal clipboard: copy, cut, paste, with the adjustment of relative references.
  • Sorting the rows of the selection, filling down, filling right and continuing a series.
  • Finding and replacing in the raw contents of the active sheet.
  • Exporting the active sheet as CSV text into a text area, and importing a CSV text from it.
  • An undo and redo history, one for the whole workbook.
  • Saving the workbook in localStorage and restoring it when the page loads.

Out of scope:

  • More than five sheets, sheets of another size, moving or copying a whole sheet.
  • Files: nothing is read from or written to the disk; CSV is exchanged through a text area.
  • The system clipboard: copy, cut and paste use a clipboard internal to the page.
  • Editing inside the cell itself: content is typed in the formula bar.
  • The current date and time, times of day, time zones, date writings other than YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Currencies, fonts, colors and borders chosen per cell, column widths, merged cells, filtering, charts, printing.
  • Any control not described in this specification.

1.3 User

One user, on a desktop or a phone browser, using mouse, touch or keyboard.

1.4 Operating environment

  • A current version of Chromium.
  • The page is opened directly from the file system or from any static file server.
  • Viewport widths from 360 px to 1920 px.
  • localStorage is normally available; the page shall also work when it is not (FR-PER-04).

1.5 Design and implementation constraints

  • One file named index.html. All HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG are inline. See NFR-PKG-01.
  • No library, framework, font or image loaded from the network. See NFR-NET-01.
  • No alert, confirm or prompt: every question and every message has its field, its button or its message element in the page. See NFR-DLG-01.
  • No uncaught script error. See NFR-ERR-01.
  • The data-testid values, attributes, accessible names and texts of 03-ui-contract are part of the interface and shall be used exactly as written.
  • The visual design is free within 08-rendering.
  • The technology is free: any language features, structure and techniques that run in the single delivered file.

1.6 Assumptions

  • The page title and the page's visible brand title are set by the instructions that accompany this specification, not by this document.
