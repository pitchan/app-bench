8. Rendering requirements

The visual design is free. These requirements set the limits every design shall respect. They are checked at two viewports: narrow 360 × 740 and wide 1280 × 800, in the light color scheme.

The checked states are:

State How it is reached
Load The page has loaded.
Filled Every cell of rows 1 to 6 holds the text Long label for a narrow cell. Every cell of rows 7 to 12 holds the number 123456789.125 with the format fixed2 (shown 123,456,789.13). Then B2 holds =1/0, and the selection is B2:D8 with B2 active: it holds texts, one error and six numbers, so selection-summary is visible and reads `Sum: 740740734.75
Editing The Filled state, then the user fills formula-input with =SUM(A1:H12) without committing.

A control is any toolbar control and formula-input.

The grid may scroll inside a container of its own. Such a container is not the window: the requirements below state where each applies.

DR-LAY-01 No horizontal scrolling

  • Statement: In every checked state at both viewports, the system shall not make the page wider than the viewport: document.documentElement.scrollWidth shall not exceed window.innerWidth.
  • Rationale: A phone user never scrolls the whole page sideways; long contents stay inside their cell or inside the scrolling area of the grid.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Filled sheet on a phone
  Given the viewport is 360 × 740
  When the sheet is in the Filled state
  Then the page is not wider than the viewport

DR-LAY-02 Reachable controls and cells

  • Statement: In every checked state at both viewports, each control shall be reachable: at one at least of the three window scroll positions below, the center of the control's bounding box is inside the viewport and document.elementFromPoint at that center returns the control or one of its descendants.

    1. The position reached by element.scrollIntoView({ block: "center" }) on the control.
    2. The top of the page (window.scrollTo(0, 0)).
    3. The bottom of the page (window.scrollTo(0, document.documentElement.scrollHeight)).

    In every checked state at both viewports, each of the 96 cells shall be reachable: after element.scrollIntoView({ block: "center", inline: "center" }) on the cell, the center of its bounding box is inside the viewport and document.elementFromPoint at that center returns the cell or one of its descendants.

  • Rationale: No control and no cell is covered by another element or kept out of the screen, whatever sticky or fixed parts the design uses.

  • Verification: Test

  • Acceptance criteria:

Scenario: Last cell on a phone
  Given the viewport is 360 × 740
  When the sheet is in the Filled state
  Then cell-H12 is reachable
  And delete-col is reachable

DR-FOC-01 Visible keyboard focus

  • Statement: When a control that is not disabled, or grid, receives the focus from the Tab key (Tab or Shift+Tab), the system shall make that focus visible: the element shall differ from its unfocused state in at least one of its computed outline-style, outline-width, outline-color, box-shadow, border-top-color or background-color. For grid, the difference may be on the grid element or on the element of the active cell.
  • Rationale: A keyboard user always sees where the focus is.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Focus on a button
  Given the sheet is in the Filled state
  When the user moves the focus with Tab to copy-button
  Then copy-button looks different from its unfocused state

Scenario: Focus on the grid
  Given a new sheet
  When the user moves the focus with Tab to grid
  Then grid or cell-A1 looks different from its unfocused state

DR-CON-01 Text contrast

  • Statement: For the text of each element below, in every checked state at both viewports, the contrast ratio between the computed color and the effective background shall be at least 4.5:1, computed with the WCAG 2.2 formula. The effective background of an element is obtained by blending, from the root down to the element, the computed background-color of each ancestor and of the element itself, starting from white. When the computed color has an alpha below 1, it is first blended over the effective background, and the ratio is computed between the resulting opaque color and the effective background. For each of these elements, walk from the element itself up through its ancestors to the root, and stop at the first node whose computed background-color has an alpha of 1 (walk to the root if there is none). Every node visited, the stopping node included, shall have a computed background-image of none. Nodes above the stopping node may have any background-image. The element and all of its ancestors shall have a computed opacity of 1. Disabled toolbar controls are exempt. Only the listed elements that are visible (README, section 2.5) in the checked state are checked.
Elements
every cell-<address> element that shows a text, whether it is active, in the selection, or neither, error cells included
every col-header-<column> and row-header-<row>
name-box, selection-summary
formula-input and format-select
the text of every toolbar button that is not disabled
  • Rationale: Every value and every action stays readable, selected or not.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Selected cells readable
  When the sheet is in the Filled state
  Then the contrast of cell-B2 is at least 4.5:1
  And the contrast of cell-D5 is at least 4.5:1
  And the contrast of cell-H12 is at least 4.5:1
  And the contrast of selection-summary is at least 4.5:1

DR-ANI-01 Finite animations

  • Statement: The system may run animations with an infinite iteration count only on decorative elements that carry no data-testid, contain no control and contain none of the elements listed in DR-CON-01. Every other animation and transition shall finish within 1 second of the event that starts it.
  • Rationale: Values are read on a settled page; a page that never settles cannot be read.
  • Verification: Test
  • Acceptance criteria:
Scenario: Settled after a click
  When the user clicks cell C3
  Then within 1 second no finite animation or transition is running
