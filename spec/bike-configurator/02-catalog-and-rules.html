2. Catalog and business rules

2.1 Glossary

Term Definition
Configuration The current choices: rider height, frame, wheels, drivetrain, color, accessories, quantity and applied promo code.
Option group One of: frame, wheels, drivetrain, color, accessories. Frame, wheels, drivetrain and color are single-choice groups; accessories is a multiple-choice group.
Option card The clickable card that represents one option of a group (DR-CMP-02).
Valid height A height that satisfies FR-FIT-02.
Recommended frame The frame given by section 2.3 for a valid height.
Automatic frame mode The state in which the frame follows the recommended frame (FR-FIT-04). It is on when the page loads and after FR-ORD-05; it ends at the first manual frame choice (FR-FIT-05), including activating the card of the frame already selected.
Cascade A change made by the system to another option as a consequence of the user's change (FR-OPT-04, FR-OPT-07, FR-OPT-08).
Cents An amount expressed as an integer number of euro cents. €12.50 is 1250 cents.
Today The browser's current local date.

2.3 Frame recommendation

For a valid height h, the recommended frame is the frame whose rider height range in the frames table contains h. The ranges are inclusive at both ends and cover 150 to 200 without gap.

2.4 Price calculation

All amounts are integer cents. Rounding uses round half up: a non-negative exact value x is rounded to the integer ⌊x + ½⌋. Percentages are computed on exact values, then rounded once. Floating-point arithmetic shall not be used for money.

For a configuration with a frame selected:

Step Name Formula
1 Unit price frame price + wheels surcharge + drivetrain surcharge + color surcharge (rule of 2.2 applied) + sum of the prices of the selected accessories
2 Subtotal unit price × quantity
3 Quantity discount if quantity ≥ 3: round half up of (subtotal × 5 / 100); otherwise 0
4 After quantity discount subtotal − quantity discount
5 Promo discount SPRING10 active: round half up of (after quantity discount × 10 / 100). WELCOME50 active: 5000. Otherwise 0.
6 Merchandise after quantity discount − promo discount
7 Shipping 0 if FREESHIP is the applied code, or if merchandise ≥ 200000; otherwise 4900
8 Total merchandise + shipping
9 VAT included round half up of (total × 20 / 120)

The VAT included is informative: it is part of the total, not added to it.

2.5 Delivery calculation

  1. Lead time = the drivetrain's lead time, plus 3 if the color is red, plus 2 if the quantity is 3 or more.
  2. Delivery date: starting from the day after today, count business days one by one; the delivery date is the business day whose count equals the lead time. Today itself is never counted, whatever day it is.
